Privacy Policy

What we collect. When you send an inquiry we collect your name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in and your message. When you leave feedback we collect your name, a contact email or phone number, your star rating and your comments.

How we use it. We use this information only to respond to your inquiry, provide quotes, service and support, and (for feedback) to display your name, rating and comment on our Testimonials page. Your contact details are never shown publicly.

Notifications. New inquiries are forwarded to our team by email and WhatsApp so we can respond quickly.

AI assistant. Messages you type into the "Aqua Assistant" chat are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a reply. Please do not share sensitive personal information in the chat. Chat history is kept only in your browser tab and is cleared when you close it.

Sharing. We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers needed to operate the site and when required by law.

Your choices. You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you submitted by contacting us using the details in the footer.
